◆   Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

◆
Do I need an account to use CineMatch?

Yes — you need a free account to access the home page, movie details, wishlist, and recommendations. Registration only takes a few seconds and requires just a username, email, and password.

Where does the movie data come from?

All movie data — posters, descriptions, ratings, genres, cast, trailers — is fetched live from The Movie Database (TMDB). This means results are always up-to-date with the latest releases.

How do I add a movie to my Wishlist?

Click on any movie from the home page, search results, genre page, or recommendations page to open its detail view. Then click the gold "Add to Wishlist" button below the poster. The button toggles — click again to remove.

Will my wishlist be there next time I log in?

Yes. Your wishlist is saved to your account in the database. As long as you log in with the same credentials, all your saved films will be there waiting for you.

How do movie recommendations work?

Type a movie title on the Recommendations page and CineMatch searches TMDB for it, then calls TMDB's recommendation API on that film's ID. The results are films with high similarity scores based on genre, cast, and audience overlap.

Can I update or delete my account?

Yes. Go to your Profile page — you can update your username, email, and bio. There is also a delete account option that will permanently remove your account and all your wishlist data.
